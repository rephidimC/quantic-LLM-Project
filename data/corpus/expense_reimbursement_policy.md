# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Reimbursable
Travel, meals, equipment, client entertainment.

## 2. Non-Reimbursable
Personal entertainment, family travel, fines.

## 3. Receipts
Required > $25; submit in 30 days.

## 4. Corporate Card
Misuse may lead to termination.

## 5. Travel Standards
Economy flights < 6 hrs; standard hotels.
