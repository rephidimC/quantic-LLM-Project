# Employee Handbook Overview

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to Northstar Dynamics, Inc. This handbook outlines the core values, employment expectations, and workplace standards that guide all employees. The company is committed to creating a safe, ethical, and productive work environment.

## 2. Company Values
- Integrity
- Collaboration
- Innovation
- Customer Obsession
- Accountability

## 3. Employment Classifications
Employees are categorized as:
- **Full-time**: 30+ hours weekly
- **Part-time**: < 30 hours weekly
- **Temporary/Contract**: Project-based or short-term assignments
- **Intern**: Educational or early-career training roles

Exempt vs. Non-Exempt classification adheres to FLSA guidelines.

## 4. Probationary Period
All new employees undergo a **90-day probationary period** during which job performance, cultural fit, and training completion are evaluated.

## 5. Work Hours & Attendance
Standard work hours are **9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (local time)** with flexibility provisions approved by managers. Excessive unexcused absences may result in disciplinary action.

## 6. Performance Reviews
Employees receive:
- **Semiannual performance reviews**
- Annual compensation reviews (subject to budget and performance)
- Structured career development goals

## 7. Anti-Discrimination & Equal Opportunity
The company prohibits discrimination based on age, race, gender, religion, disability, or any protected category as defined by federal and state law.

## 8. Harassment Policy
Harassment of any kind—verbal, physical, or digital—is strictly prohibited. Employees must report concerns to HR or a manager immediately.

## 9. Whistleblower Protection
Employees who report violations of policy or law in good faith are protected from retaliation.

## 10. Amendments
The company reserves the right to revise policies at any time. Employees will be notified of updates.
